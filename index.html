Terms & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here is exactly how Pomodorw handles your data:

  • Camera access — Used only for real-time face detection to track focus. Video is processed entirely on your device. No frames, images, or video are ever transmitted, uploaded, or stored anywhere.
  • Face data — All face detection runs locally in your browser using AI models loaded from a CDN. No facial data, landmarks, or expressions leave your computer.
  • Local storage — We use localStorage only to remember your settings (bell tone, voice preference, custom phrases, alert sensitivity). No cookies, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Third-party services — Google Fonts (typefaces) and jsDelivr CDN (face detection models) may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type) when resources are fetched. These are outside our control.
  • No accounts — No sign-up, no login, no user profile. There is nothing to identify you.
